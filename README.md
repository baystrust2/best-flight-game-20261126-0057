Fly a little prop courier plane over Meridian Bay: thread the glowing rings, mind your fuel, and bring her home onto runway 27. Three sorties — Ring Run, Precision Landing, and a Storm Courier run with crosswinds and rain — each with its own goals, scoring and weather. Pure canvas 2D, no libraries, no network; every sound is synthesized in WebAudio.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.

## Play

- **↑ / ↓** pitch · **← / →** bank and turn · **W / S** throttle up / down
- **G** landing gear · **F** flaps · **Space** airbrake · **P** pause · **M** mute · **R** restart
- Mouse: click the mission cards on the title screen, drag the throttle slider in flight.

Land soft, on the centreline, with the gear down — or pay for it in damage. Run out of fuel and you glide: pick your field.
